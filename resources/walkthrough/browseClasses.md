# Browse classes

The **GemStone Explorer** — its own icon in the activity bar (the far-left strip)
— is the primary way to read and edit code. It has stacked panes: **Dictionaries**,
**Classes**, and **Methods**.

Click a class in the **Classes** pane and its methods appear in the **Methods**
pane below — that pane is where you navigate between a class's methods. Click a
method to open its source.

In a hurry? **Find Class…** — `Cmd/Ctrl + K` then `C` — jumps straight to any
class by name and reveals it in the Explorer, without scrolling the tree.
